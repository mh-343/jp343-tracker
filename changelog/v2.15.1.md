## Fixes

- **Player without a file**: An open MPC-HC with nothing loaded now shows as connected and idle instead of an error asking you to check the Web Interface settings.
- **Session saving**: A session is saved as soon as you stop, close the file, quit the player or reach the end of the video, instead of minutes later. Pausing keeps it open.

## Improvements

- **Live player card**: While MPC-HC plays, the popup shows the file, a running timer and the status, today's total keeps counting, and the toolbar badge shows recording.
- **Settings status**: The MPC-HC status line in the settings updates on its own while the page is open.
- **Session labels**: Platform names in the popup session list are written out, for example YouTube and MPC-HC.
