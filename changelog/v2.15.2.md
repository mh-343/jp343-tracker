## Fixes

- **YouTube live links**: Videos opened through a youtube.com/live/ link are now tracked like regular videos, including original titles and the difficulty chip.
- **Playlist tiles**: Original titles are now only applied to single videos, so playlist and collection tiles keep their own names.

## Improvements

- **Difficulty vote**: The top answer now reads "Almost all", which fits videos where you followed everything but a few words were new.
