## Features

- **Active/passive tagging**: Each session now records whether the video tab was actually in front of you or playing in the background. The This Week and Monthly charts show the split, tags can be pinned in the popup or corrected later in the history, and a setting hides the display if you prefer plain stats. With account sync, tags also appear in your My Hub.

## Fixes

- **Firefox performance**: Long YouTube sessions no longer build up slowdowns or freezes in Firefox-based browsers.
- **Custom day start**: Right after signing in, today's total no longer briefly counts toward the wrong day.
