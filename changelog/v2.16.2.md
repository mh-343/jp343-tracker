## Fixes

- **YouTube channel projects**: When you opened a video from the search results or went on to the next video, the session could be saved under the channel of the page you came from and create a project for the wrong channel. The channel now comes from the video that is actually playing.
- **YouTube channel name**: In rare cases a button text such as "Join" could be saved as the channel name. Only the channel name itself is used now.
- **Popup totals**: The total of grouped sessions from jp343.com in the popup, for example "(3×)", added up rounded minutes and could differ from the dashboard. It now adds the exact seconds, like the dashboard.
- **Logout**: Logging out in the dashboard showed no progress while waiting for jp343.com, and a second click sent a second request. The button now shows "Logging out…", ignores repeat clicks and logs you out on this device after a few seconds even if jp343.com does not answer.

## Improvements

- **MPC-HC in Firefox**: The MPC-HC settings now say that Firefox needs MPC-HC 2.1.0 or newer, because older versions do not let the browser read the player status.
