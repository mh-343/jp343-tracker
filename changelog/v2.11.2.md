## Fixes

- **Firefox for Android**: History and statistics load reliably again. They could stay empty or show outdated numbers for a while after signing in.
- **Statistics after deleting**: Deleting a session now updates your totals correctly. Repeating a delete no longer subtracts the same session twice, and a total can no longer drop below zero.
- **Switching accounts**: Signing out or switching to another account no longer brings the previous account's sessions back into view.
- **Japanese-only filter**: Chinese videos are no longer mistaken for Japanese. A Chinese description could previously make a video count as Japanese content and keep it tracked.
- **Session details**: Moving to the next video no longer stamps its title, channel or thumbnail onto the session you just finished.
- **Popup statistics**: Time logged on the website or another device now shows up in the popup right away instead of on the next refresh.
