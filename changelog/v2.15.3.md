## Fixes

- **Allowed channels**: An allowed channel could suddenly stop being tracked in Japanese-only mode and show up twice in your list after you allowed it again. Allowed and blocked channels are now recognized in both forms YouTube uses for a channel, which should put an end to this.
- **Channel lists while signed out**: Allowing or blocking a channel while signed out could wipe your other allowed and blocked channels. Your lists now stay as they are, and they are sent to your account once you sign in.
- **Removed channels coming back**: A channel you had just removed could reappear a moment later. It now stays removed.
- **Streak warning**: The "streak at risk" notification could show a much shorter streak than you actually have, or appear even though you had already logged time today on another device. It now goes by your account's streak and today's minutes across all your devices.

## Improvements

- **Popup channel buttons**: The popup now shows Allowed or Blocked even when a channel is listed under its other form, so you are not offered a second Allow, and one click removes the channel completely.
- **Website sessions**: Sessions from jp343.com (reader, podcast, local audio) used to appear as plain ids in the dashboard and popup lists; they now have their own name, icon and color.
