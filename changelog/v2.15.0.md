## Features

- **MPC-HC tracking**: Track Japanese videos you watch in the MPC-HC desktop player. Turn it on in the dashboard settings, enable MPC-HC's Web Interface on port 13579, and playback is counted while your browser stays open. Desktop only, opt-in.
- **Sync issues in the popup**: The popup now shows sessions that need sync attention, with the reason (for example sign-in needed, a retry waiting, or a session over 24 hours) and buttons to retry, sign in, or remove a local entry.
- **Account reading stats**: When you are signed in, the dashboard reading card shows your reading time and speed from your whole account, not just this browser, and labels which one it is showing.

## Fixes

- **Backup import**: Importing a backup keeps your lifetime total instead of lowering it, and recomputes your streak from the combined days rather than copying the backup's value.

## Improvements

- **Website deletions**: Deleting a session on jp343.com now removes it from the extension as well and adjusts your local totals.
- **Live today total**: The popup keeps today's total updating while a session is running.
- **Session start times**: Pending sessions in the popup show when the session started.
