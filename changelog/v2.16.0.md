## Features

- **GameSentenceMiner tracking**: Visual novels and games you read with GameSentenceMiner now count as reading time, including the characters you read. Turn it on in the dashboard settings while GameSentenceMiner runs on the same computer; it works without an account, and your sessions are sent once you sign in. Desktop only, opt-in.
- **Older GameSentenceMiner history**: You can also import reading sessions from before you turned the tracking on, from the same settings panel.

## Fixes

- **Skipping ahead**: Jumping a few seconds ahead while a video was playing, for example with the arrow keys, used to add the skipped seconds to your watched time. Now only the time you actually played is counted.
- **Long channel names**: Sessions from YouTube channels with very long names could fail to sync and keep retrying. The name is now shortened before it is sent.

## Improvements

- **Sync retry**: When a session cannot be sent, the popup now shows the last error, and Retry sends it right away instead of waiting for the next attempt.
- **Mokuro series**: Mokuro volumes are now sent together with their series, so jp343.com can keep the volumes of one manga together.
- **ttu reader books**: A book you read in ttu reader keeps one entry on jp343.com across all your devices, and your reading position in the book is saved with it.
- **Finished volumes**: Volumes and books you finished before this update are reported once, so they show as finished on jp343.com.
