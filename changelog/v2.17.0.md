## Fixes

- **Custom site series**: On custom sites whose links carry the episode at the end, such as "?ep=3", different series could all land in one project called "watch". Each series now gets its own project, and links that already worked keep their project.
- **Muted videos on custom sites**: A video on a custom site that started muted was not tracked until you turned the sound on, and the muted part was lost. Muted videos now count once they have played for 30 seconds, including those 30 seconds, and pausing, muting and resuming continues the session right away instead of after several seconds.

## Improvements

- **Active and passive time**: Switching to another tab or window during a video used to cut the session into separate active and passive pieces. A session now stays one entry that counts how much of it was active and how much passive, and marking it active or passive applies to the whole session.
- **Short look-aways**: Time spent on another tab or window during a video used to turn passive after 20 seconds. Now it counts as active if you come back within 90 seconds; longer breaks count as passive.
- **Podcasts during videos**: When you started a podcast on jp343.com while the extension tracked a video or Spotify in the same browser, both counted at the same time without any notice. The podcast now pauses and says why, and "Play anyway" keeps it playing for that video if you want both.
