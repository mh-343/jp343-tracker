## Features

- **Sessions full view**: A new button next to "Sessions" in the popup opens a full view with many more of your recent sessions. When you are signed in, the popup loads more sessions from jp343.com, and "Full history on jp343.com" opens all of them in My Hub.

## Fixes

- **Platform toggles**: Turning a platform off in Settings > Platforms did not stop tracking. It now does, and a running session is saved first. Because the old switches had no effect, all platforms start out on after this update, so check Settings > Platforms if you had turned one off.
- **Ad notice in the popup**: The popup did not show "Ad detected - paused" during ads. It now appears below the timer.

## Improvements

- **Popup session card**: The channel strip and the timer row took room away from your sessions. The channel or title now sits in the session card, the timer next to Pause and Stop & Save, and the popup gets shorter while the sessions list is collapsed.
- **Reading time limit**: Mokuro and ttsu reader stopped counting after 12 hours of reading on one day. That limit is gone.
