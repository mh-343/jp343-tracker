## Fixes

- **Mokuro and ttsu characters**: When you paged back in Mokuro or ttsu reader and then read on, the pages you read again were counted a second time. That made your character count and reading speed too high. Characters now count only past the furthest point you reached in a volume. If you start a series over or mark a volume as unread in Mokuro, or delete your statistics in ttsu reader, the new read still counts from the start.
- **Mokuro and ttsu reading time**: Reading time could go missing when you paged back in Mokuro or ttsu reader. That time now counts.

## Improvements

- **Reading speed**: The dashboard showed one speed in characters per minute for all reading apps together, so switching between manga and novels pushed it up and down. Now it shows characters per hour for the app and medium you read most, for example "Text chars/h". This is the same value as in My Hub on jp343.com. Hover over it to see the app, your reading days and your usual range. The speed needs five reading days with at least 10 minutes each and two hours in total. Until then it shows a dash, and hovering shows what is still missing. This also works without an account, for Mokuro and ttsu reader in this browser. Recently read titles show characters per hour too.
- **Podcasts during MPC-HC**: When you started a podcast on jp343.com while the extension tracked a video in MPC-HC, both counted at the same time without any notice. The podcast now pauses and says why, as it does for videos in the browser, and "Play anyway" keeps it playing for that video if you want both. With MPC-HC this can take up to half a minute, in Firefox up to a minute, because the extension checks the player at that interval.
