## Features

- **Twitch VODs**: Watching VODs and past broadcasts now counts toward your time, just like live streams.

## Improvements

- **Spotify music tracking**: Music is now off by default on new installs and can be turned on with the Music chip in the popup or in the dashboard settings. Podcast and audiobook tracking, and all existing installs, are unchanged.
