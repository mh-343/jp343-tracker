## Features

- **Reading completions**: Finishing a book in Mokuro or the ttu reader now marks it complete in your account automatically.

## Improvements

- **Custom sites in embedded players**: Custom sites now track video that plays inside embedded and nested frames, not just top-level players.
- **Custom site accuracy**: Sessions start only when the video actually advances, and idle gaps no longer inflate the tracked time.
- **Startup reliability**: Tracking still initializes even if a rare startup step fails.

## Fixes

- **Custom sites on subdomains**: Sites served from a subdomain are no longer silently skipped.
- **YouTube collaborations**: Videos posted as collaborations now group under the right channel instead of separate one-off projects.
