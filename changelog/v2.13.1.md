## Fixes

- **Original YouTube titles**: When YouTube keeps overriding the original title, the extension now backs off instead of fighting for it, which should remove the slowdowns and freezes some Firefox users saw.
- **Bulk retag**: Marking all untagged sessions as active or passive now covers every untagged session, not just the most recent ones.
- **Active share on podcast days**: The weekly active share now shows up even on days with a lot of podcast listening, since podcasts have no active or passive tag.
- **Session history on mobile**: Duration and active/passive labels no longer overlap on narrow screens.

## Improvements

- **Chart colors**: Active, passive and podcast segments use distinct colors instead of shades of the theme color, so the split is readable on dark backgrounds.
- **Podcast segment**: Podcast listening shows as its own segment in the This Week and Monthly charts.
- **Tap breakdown**: Tapping or clicking a chart bar shows the minutes per segment below the chart, which also works on touch devices.
